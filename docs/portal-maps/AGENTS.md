\# Portal Maps Agent Instructions



\## Scope



These instructions apply to `docs/portal-maps`.



Portal maps document observed Finale Health portal behavior, selectors, navigation paths, page quirks, and known failure modes.



\## Documentation Requirements



When documenting a portal area, include:



\- Portal section name.

\- URL or route pattern if safe to describe.

\- Entry point from the dashboard or patient chart.

\- Key visible labels, headings, buttons, and tables.

\- Stable selector assumptions.

\- Known dynamic behavior.

\- Failure symptoms.

\- Recovery or fallback behavior.

\- Related automation files.



\## Safety



\- Do not include real credentials.

\- Do not include unredacted patient data.

\- Use sanitized examples for patient names, MRNs, document IDs, and URLs.
